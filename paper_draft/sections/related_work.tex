\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\para{Representation-based detection.} \citet{quaremba2026linear} show that residual activations support sample-efficient linear machine-text detection and transfer across domains and generators. \textsc{SV-Detect} similarly uses layerwise logistic directions as features for robust detection under distribution shift \cite{vishnyakov2026svdetect}. Both results support a continuous and linearly accessible authorship signal, but neither adds the fitted separator during generation. Our study makes that intervention the primary test.

Detection datasets also shape what such a signal can mean. \hcThree pairs human and ChatGPT answers across domains \cite{guo2023close}; \mage collects broad in- and out-of-distribution machine-text data \cite{li2024mage}; and \raid stresses detectors across generators, decoding settings, domains, and attacks \cite{dugan2024raid}. These benchmarks show that detector performance can change sharply under distribution shift. We therefore train the lexical outcome detector outside \hape, validate both outcomes on \mage and \raid, and require agreement before making a strong causal claim.

\para{Activation steering and style.} Contrastive activation addition and representation engineering show that residual-stream interventions can control high-level behavior at inference time \cite{turner2023steering,zou2023representation}. Human evaluation of style vectors further shows that moderate interventions can change perceived style while stronger ones can reduce quality \cite{diallo2026effectiveness}. \citet{kuznetsov2025feature} identify sparse-autoencoder features related to formality, repetition, complexity, and tone in artificial-text detection and causally steer individual features. These findings motivate signed doses, quality measures, and style controls. Unlike feature-level steering, we test the dense supervised separator itself.

\para{Persona and self-authorship.} The Assistant Axis is a dominant, steerable persona direction that is present in base models and repositioned by post-training \cite{lu2026assistant}. A detector could label polished Assistant prose as machine-written, so we estimate same-model Assistant and formality axes and remove their joint span from the authorship vector. \citet{ackerman2025inspection} causally control whether Llama-3-8B-Instruct claims or perceives self-authorship. Their result is the closest mechanistic precedent, but changing a model's answer to ``did I write this?'' is distinct from changing the surface properties judged by an independent observer.

\para{Content-paired human and machine writing.} \citet{reinhart2025llms} introduce content-aligned corpora and show persistent grammatical and rhetorical differences between human and model continuations, with larger differences for instruction-tuned than base Llama variants. Their \hape structure lets us hold the prefix and document identity fixed. We use it both to estimate paired authorship directions and to compare base versus instruction-tuned Qwen checkpoints without treating topic as authorship.
