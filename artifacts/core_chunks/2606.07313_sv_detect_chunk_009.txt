G Additional comparison with
RepreGuard
RepreGuard (Chen et al., 2025) is the closest
representation-based AI-generated text detection
method to SV-Detect.
Table 7 reports results of RepreGuard under our
DetectRL evaluation protocol using the GPT-Neo-
2.7B backbone. However, the default backbone
of RepreGuard is LLaMA-3.1-8B. In this section,
we provide an additional comparison of SV-Detect
to RepreGuard under the matched backbones us-
ing the default setup of Chen et al. (2025): 512-
pair train/test cells with per-LLM stratification, and
macro-averaging across a small number of boot-
strap seeds. We use RAID (Dugan et al., 2024) for
comparison, which is the default benchmark used
in Chen et al. (2025).
Table 15 reports RAID performance for Repre-
Guard and SV-Detect trained on RAID official
training split, under RepreGuard’s training proto-
col, at both GPT-Neo-2.7B and LLaMA-3.1-8B-
Instruct. We follow the aggregation of Dugan
et al. (2024) and macro-average each row across
the eight open-source RAID configurations (Chat
vs. Non-Chat× greedy vs. sampling× repetition-
penalty on/off).
Results suggest that RepreGuard is highly
backbone-sensitive: at GPT-Neo-2.7B its RAID
score collapses to near-chance (Chat 4.5%
TPR@5%, Non-Chat 5.3%; AUROC≈0.5 ), and
only moving to LLaMA-3.1-8B-Instruct recov-
ers Chat performance ( 92.9% TPR@5%). Non-
Chat generators remain hard for RepreGuard even
at LLaMA-8B ( 41.7%). The same backbone-
sensitivity is visible on DetectRL Multi-LLM: our
reproduction of RepreGuard under its own pro-
tocol yields 2.5% TPR@5% (AUROC 0.366) at
GPT-Neo-2.7B and 30.3% TPR@5% (AUROC
0.812) at LLaMA-3.1-8B-Instruct, macro-averaged
across the four Multi-LLM generators. SV-Detect
does not exhibit this dependence: at both GPT-
Neo-2.7B and LLaMA-3.1-8B it reaches≈99.7%
TPR@5% /≈1.0 AUROC on both Chat and
Non-Chat RAID cells. In other words, SV-Detect
matches or beats RepreGuard at itsbestbackbone
while remaining strong at the small GPT-Neo back-
bone where RepreGuard collapses. This supports
our claim that the LogReg-based steering construc-
tion produces a discriminative direction that is ro-
bust to backbone choice, whereas RepreGuard’s
cluster-separation heuristic degrades sharply when
